## The 11 historical cases (H1' dataset — NR-020 + NR-021)

### 1. Titius-Bode law (1772) — FAILED
Numerical progression `a=0.4+0.3×2ⁿ`, fit to 6-7 known planetary distances,
failed at Neptune's discovery (1846).
- en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titius–Bode_law
- "The Titius-Bode Law Revisited But Not Revived" — arXiv:0806.3532
- Modern exoplanet-system replication attempts (contested, excluded from
  the regression as an ambiguous outcome): arXiv:1602.02877, MNRAS
  538(4):2730 (2025)

### 2. Eddington's exact α⁻¹=137 (1929-1944) — FAILED
- "On Arthur Eddington's Theory of Everything" (Kragh) — arXiv:1510.04046
- "On D. Gross' criticism of S. Eddington..." — sciencedirect
  S0960077906010149
- "Attempts at a determination of the fine-structure constant from first
  principles: A brief historical overview" (Jentschura & Nandori, 2014) —
  arXiv:1411.4673 — broader historical review of many such attempts

### 3. Dirac Large Numbers Hypothesis (1937) — FAILED

## Meta-literature (the "numerology vs. real physics" distinction itself)

- **Amir, Lemeshko, Tokieda (2016)**, "Surprises in numerical expressions
  of physical constants" — arXiv:1603.00299 — the base-rate/null-model
  paper: how often "surprisingly simple" constant matches arise by pure
  chance in a wide search space. Used directly in NR-020's Repair
  Hypothesis construction.
- **Martin J. Rees (2004)**, "Numerical coincidences and 'tuning' in
  cosmology" — arXiv:astro-ph/0401424 — Hoyle's carbon-oxygen resonance
  and other cosmological coincidences, broader context.
- "On the vital difference between number theory and numerology in
  physics" — sciencedirect S0960077908003469
- "Sommerfeld's Miracle" (Vickers, philosophy of science) —
  thebsps.org/auxhyp/sommerfelds-miracle-the-ultimate-challenge-to-
  scientific-realism-peter-vickers — Sommerfeld's 1916 formula matching
  Dirac's 1928 result for the wrong reasons ("fortuitous computational
  accident," per Heisenberg/Schrödinger) — excluded from the regression
  as structurally different (not a "spotted pattern" claim, a derivation
  with a compensating error) but genuinely relevant background; see
  also arXiv:2401.07485 ("The Sommerfeld Puzzle and Its Extensions").
- ScienceAmerican/phys.org coverage of Garrett Lisi's "An Exceptionally
  Simple Theory of Everything" (2007) — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An_
  Exceptionally_Simple_Theory_of_Everything — excluded from the
  regression (framework-level, not a simple algebraic identity) but a
  well-documented modern FAILED case if this dataset is ever extended.
- SU(5) GUT proton-decay prediction, ruled out by Super-Kamiokande —
  arXiv:hep-ex/9904020, hep.bu.edu/~superk/pdk.html — excluded from the
  regression (many-parameter GUT framework, not a simple formula) but a
  clean, well-documented FAILED prediction if extended.

## Belle II tau-mass measurement (used in `CONSILIENCE_eq32.md`)

- **Belle II Collaboration (2023)**, "Measurement of the τ-lepton mass
  with the Belle II experiment" — arXiv:2305.19116, Phys. Rev. D 108,
  032006, doi/10.1103/PhysRevD.108.032006 — m_τ = 1777.09 ± 0.08 (stat)
  ± 0.11 (syst) MeV. This is the genuinely independent data point Eq.32's
  own m_τ=1776.840 MeV prediction was checked against (1.84σ tension).
- PDG world average: m_τ = 1776.86 ± 0.12 MeV — NOT independent for this
  purpose (likely close to/including the value Eq.32 was originally
  anchored against).
- DESY announcement of Belle II Run 2 (data-taking since 2024-02-20) —
  desy.de/news — the dated future event pearled for re-checking the
  1.84σ tension.
