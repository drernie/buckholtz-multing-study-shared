## Falsifiable predicate and pre-registered MCID

**Predicate under test:** does the disagreement magnitude cluster by
*which proxy pair is compared* — specifically, do comparisons involving a
caustic/velocity-dispersion-based estimator (the family `P196`-`P201`'s
own Girardi/caustic line belongs to) show systematically larger
disagreement than lensing-anchored X-ray/SZ comparisons?

**MCID (pre-registered before reading the numbers below):** MATERIAL
refinement requires (a) the caustic-involving group's mean disagreement
to exceed the non-caustic (WL-anchored) group's by a factor ≥2, AND (b)
no more than 1 real counter-example in either direction. Fewer than that
→ the data is too mixed to support a clean sub-rule; report the honest
spread instead of forcing a pattern.

## Method

Real, cited `arXiv` search (2 targeted queries, 12 results), abstracts
read in full for 5 additional independent, real, published comparisons

## Data — all entries real, `[VERIFIED-arXiv]`

### Group A — lensing-anchored (WL vs X-ray hydrostatic, WL vs SZ), no caustic involved

| Source | Comparison | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Applegate et al. 2015 (`arXiv:1509.02162`, Weighing the Giants IV) | WL vs X-ray hydrostatic, `r_2500`, massive relaxed clusters | `M_lensing/M_X = 0.96 ± 9%(stat) ± 9%(sys)` — `4%` bias |
| Simet et al. 2015 (`arXiv:1502.01024`) | WL vs X-ray (REFLEX+BCS, 166 clusters) | `⟨M_X/M_WL⟩ = 0.75 ± 0.07(stat) ± 0.05(sys)` — `25%` bias |
| Hoekstra et al. 2012 (`arXiv:1208.0606`, Canadian Cluster Comparison) | WL vs SZ (50 clusters, `r_2500`) | intrinsic scatter `12±5%`, no strong bias reported |
| Muñoz-Echeverría et al. 2023 (`arXiv:2312.01154`) | Hydrostatic X-ray vs WL (53 clusters, `z=0.05-1.07`) | `(1-b) = 0.739^{+0.075}_{-0.070}` — `26%` bias |

### Group B — caustic/dynamical-estimator involved (the family `P196`-`P201` themselves use)

| Source | Comparison | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Diaferio, Geller & Rines 2005 (`arXiv:astro-ph/0506560`) | Caustic vs WL, 3 clusters (A2390, MS1358, Cl 0024) | "remarkably good agreement... out to `~2 Mpc/h`" — **no large disagreement**, small-N |

## Honest reading — the predicate does NOT cleanly survive

**Group A (non-caustic) spread:** `4%` to `26%` bias, `12%` scatter — a
real, moderate, well-established "hydrostatic/dynamical-state bias"
floor, consistently in the tens-of-percent range across 4 independent,
large-sample, modern studies.

**Group B (caustic-involved) spread:** `~0% (Diaferio, "remarkably good
agreement")` to `150%` (`P197`) — **not uniformly larger than Group A**.
Diaferio et al. 2005 is a real, direct counter-example: caustic vs weak
lensing, for 3 clusters, showed *closer* agreement than several Group-A
pairs. **MCID check: FAILS condition (b)** — Diaferio is exactly the
kind of counter-example the pre-registration said would block a clean
sub-rule, and it is a real, on-topic, directly-comparable measurement
(caustic vs WL specifically), not a tangential result.

**A plausible reconciling factor, named but not further tested here:**
Diaferio's 3 clusters were selected as good caustic-method test cases
(the method's own validation sample); `P197`'s 123-cluster HeCS-SZ
sample and `P200`'s 44-cluster comparison are larger, more
heterogeneous, unselected-for-relaxation samples. Sample
selection/homogeneity, not the caustic method per se, may be the real
driver of the larger disagreement in this project's own results — but
this is `[HYPOTHESIS]`, not tested by the data gathered here.

## Verdict

**MCID: NOT MET.** The clean sub-rule ("caustic-involving comparisons
systematically disagree more than lensing-anchored ones") does **not**
survive — Diaferio et al. 2005 is one real, on-topic counter-example,
which the pre-registration named as sufficient to block the refinement.

**What the meta-analysis DOES establish, per `P202`'s own original
question:** the broader literature confirms real, substantial,
well-documented disagreement between independent cluster mass proxies
generally exists (Group A alone: `4%-26%` bias is already non-trivial;
`P197`'s `150%` is not an outlier in kind, only in degree) — **this
directly reinforces `P202`'s `PART-OF-MACROSYSTEM` verdict at the
qualitative level** (disagreement is a known, field-wide phenomenon, not
unique to this project's Girardi/NFW comparison). It does **not**
support a more precise, mechanism-specific sub-claim about *which*
proxy pairs disagree most — the honest outcome is a wider, better-
sourced confirmation of the general pattern, not a sharper one.

## What this does and does NOT establish

**Does establish:** `P202`'s own qualitative verdict (macro, not local)
is now backed by 5 additional independent, real, cited literature
sources beyond the original 2 (`P197`, `P200`/Logan+2022) — a stronger
evidence base for the same conclusion.

**Does NOT establish:**
1. A refined, falsifiable sub-rule about which specific proxy families
   disagree most — the data is too mixed (Diaferio's counter-example).
2. Anything about MULTING, v82, or any TJB construction —
   `NO_AUTHOR_ERROR`, this is entirely non-MULTING cluster astrophysics
   literature, same as `P196`-`P202`.
3. That this is an exhaustive literature review — 5 papers from 2
   bounded searches, not a systematic meta-analysis with a defined
   inclusion protocol.
