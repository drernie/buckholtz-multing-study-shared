## 2. The five qualifiers (Q1–Q5) — mandatory whenever this status is
## cited, per §0

**Q1 (Atom 1 scope).** Reproduces MULTING's parametric tier *structure*
and sign pattern — **not** its fitted numerical ratio. This
construction gives `β_q/β_d = √6/2 ≈ 1.2247`; MULTING's own AI-fitted

**Q2 (Atom 2 — reformulation, not derivation).** The `(+,−,+)` sign
pattern is the binomial expansion of `(m−κk)²` — real algebra — but the
`(m−κk)` structure itself requires two matching-MULTING choices

`q` opposite-signed to mass, chosen to make `F_d` repulsive). This is a
reformulation of MULTING's own sign postulate as an equivalent bilinear
postulate, not an independent, first-principles derivation of it.

**Q2b (recomposition tension, new this review).** Mirror-symmetric
orientation is physically an *induced* polarization configuration. But
`docs/131`'s own sombrero analysis shows that for an *intrinsic* dipole
(exactly what `P1`'s action uses — `pᵢ=κkᵢrᵢ/c²` is a fixed property of
body `i`, not a gradient-response) the mirror-symmetric alignment is the
**unstable energy maximum**, not the stable minimum. `FINDING_P4`
already surfaced this mismatch; it remains unresolved here.

**Q3 (Atom 4 — narrow, not general, EP escape).** The "fifth force, not
gravity" reclassification is logically valid for `docs/131`'s
*specific* argument (it names and relaxes the assumption "`F_oP` is
pure gravity"). It does **not** establish compatibility with actual
laboratory/solar-system fifth-force bounds (MICROSCOPE, Lunar Laser
Ranging). "EP-resolved" means the narrow structural objection is
answered — not that experimental viability is established.

**Q4 (Atom 5 — P11 mechanism is a candidate, not a result).** The exact
massless-case cancellation is real. `P11`'s proposed fixed-mass
(`μ~H₀/c`) mechanism for breaking that cancellation cosmologically while
preserving it at cluster scale is an outlined candidate with two named,
unresolved items: whether the near-field `β_d=2, β_q=√6` ladder survives
a nonzero `μ` at cluster scale, and whether `μ~H₀/c` specifically gives
an observable magnitude. **Addressed below as P149.**

**Q5 (recomposition — trade-off glossed by the combined label).**
"Cosmologically null" (needs `μ=0` exactly) and "mass-scaling-corrected
cosmological signal" (needs `μ~H₀/c` nonzero) are two different

honest combined statement is conditional: null at `μ=0`; a candidate
signal only if a nonzero `μ` is shown compatible with the near-field
structure (P149) and its magnitude is checked (not yet done).
