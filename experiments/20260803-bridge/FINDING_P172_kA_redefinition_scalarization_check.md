## 1. The candidate mechanism: spontaneous scalarization

**Real, peer-reviewed, `[VERIFIED-REAL]` via direct WebSearch this
session:** the Damour–Esposito-Farèse model (and its many descendants)
predicts that a sufficiently compact self-gravitating body develops a
non-trivial scalar field configuration above a critical threshold — a
genuine phase transition (tachyonic instability, nonlinearly quenched).
The resulting **scalar charge is structure-dependent and NOT simply
proportional to the object's mass** — exactly the structural feature
`docs/131`'s residual branch #1 needs. Multiple independent sources
confirm this is a mainstream, actively-studied theory class (not a
fringe construction): [New Perspectives on Spontaneous Scalarization
in Black Holes and Neutron Stars](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.15330),
[Spontaneous scalarization](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.01766).

**Positive-energy check:** `[VERIFIED-REAL]` — scalarized solutions'
"tensor mass has natural energy-like properties: it is positive
definite, decreases monotonically by [GW] emission, and is well defined
even in dynamical spacetimes." Scalarization does not introduce negative
energy or a ghost in its standard (massless- or massive-but-not-tachyonic-
asymptotically) form — it is a real, stable, positive-energy phenomenon,
studied precisely because it is physically viable.

**EP-violation, explicitly confirmed, and a real cross-connection to
`FINDING_P171` row 3:** `[VERIFIED-REAL]` — "the definition of mass in
scalar-tensor theories is subtle, due to the fact that **these theories
violate the strong equivalence principle**." This means the
`k_A`-redefinition branch (row 1) and the EP-relaxation branch (row 3,
already checked in `FINDING_P171` against chameleon/symmetron screening)
are **not independent** — scalarization-type solutions inherently
combine both relaxations at once. `FINDING_P171`'s own ledger treated
rows 1 and 3 as separate; this file's own finding is that the most
natural mechanism connecting to row 1 requires relaxing row 3
simultaneously, not as an optional extra.

**The sign mechanism — algebraic consistency check, not an independent
derivation (corrected, skeptic-caught overclaim):** `[VERIFIED-sympy-
CONSISTENCY-CHECK, this session]` — for a canonically-normalized
(ghost-free) scalar field linearly coupled to a point charge, calibrated
to this project's own already-established convention (`docs/130`:
"stable scalar-scalar exchange is attractive" for same-sign/same-
composition sources), the interaction energy `U(r) ∝ -q₁q₂/r` gives
**same-sign charges `U<0`, opposite-sign charges `U>0`** by direct
algebra. This is a real, correctly-executed check, but it is a
near-tautological consequence of the bilinear (`∝q₁q₂`) linear-coupling
ansatz once the sign is calibrated to `docs/130`'s own already-
established fact — NOT an independent physical derivation of the
opposite-branch-repulsion claim, and the first draft's framing
("independently verified") overclaimed it. What it DOES confirm: the
opposite-sign-repulsion claim requires no assumption beyond ordinary
linear scalar coupling — a modest but genuine sanity check, correctly
scoped. Scalarized bodies generically admit a discrete pair
of degenerate branches with opposite-sign scalar charge (the underlying
`Z₂`-type symmetry of the scalarization potential, confirmed via search
on bifurcation structure — though the specific "opposite branches for
two SEPARATE, independently-evolving objects" question is addressed in
§3, not resolved by this general fact alone).

## 2. Direct search for a thermal-energy-triggered variant

**The one place this session found thermal effects entering
scalarization at all:** proto-neutron stars. `[VERIFIED-REAL]`: "the
scalar charge of proto-neutron stars at finite temperature is greater
than that of cold neutron stars" — a real, published connection between
thermal energy and scalar-charge magnitude. But two disqualifying
features for direct application to MULTING, found in the same search:
(a) temperature there **modulates** an already-compactness-triggered
effect, it does not independently trigger scalarization on its own; (b)
the paper's own finding is that "an increase in temperature softens the
equation of state, leading to an **expansion** of the star and a
**reduction in compactness**" — i.e., in this specific mechanism, more
thermal energy pushes AWAY from the scalarization threshold, the
opposite of what a "thermal energy sources repulsion" story would need.

**No source found** connecting thermal/kinetic energy to scalarization
(or any structurally similar charge-bifurcation phenomenon) at
galaxy-cluster mass/compactness scales, independent of proto-neutron-
star physics. `SOURCE_NOT_FOUND`, not evidence of impossibility.

## 3. Three separate, identified gaps (why this branch remains open)

1. **Compactness mismatch, severe and quantitative (corrected —
   skeptic-caught arithmetic error, independently re-verified by direct
   computation before applying).** Standard scalarization triggers only
   above a critical compactness (`GM/(Rc²)` of order several tenths —
   `0.207` for a typical `1.4 M☉`, `10 km` neutron star, hand-computed).
   Galaxy clusters, computed directly (`G=6.674×10⁻¹¹`, `M=10¹⁵ M☉`):
   `GM/(Rc²) = 4.8×10⁻⁵` at `R=1 Mpc`, down to `4.8×10⁻⁶` at `R=10 Mpc`
   — **roughly 4 orders of magnitude** below the neutron-star threshold
   (NOT the first draft's erroneous "10⁻⁹–10⁻⁸" / "8-9 orders of
   magnitude," off by ~3-4 orders of magnitude). The qualitative
   conclusion is unchanged by this correction — the standard mechanism,
   as published, still cannot trigger at cluster scales, and no
   alternative cluster-scale trigger was found — but the quantitative
   anchor was wrong and is now independently verified.
2. **Thermal energy is not established as an independent trigger.** The
   one literature connection found (proto-neutron stars) shows thermal
   energy modulating, not driving, scalarization — and in a direction
   unhelpful to a "more thermal energy → stronger repulsive effect"
   story, in that specific mechanism.
3. **Opposite-branch selection between separate objects is not just
   unestablished — it runs against the natural cosmological default
   (sharpened, skeptic-caught).** Even granting a hypothetical
   cluster-scale scalarization mechanism: standard DEF's `Z₂` symmetry
   (`φ→−φ`) genuinely does admit a degenerate `±`-charge branch pair in
   the idealized, exactly-symmetric theory — but in any realistic
   cosmological setting the asymptotic field value `φ_∞≠0` EXPLICITLY
   BREAKS that `Z₂` symmetry, and objects sharing a common cosmological
   environment/boundary condition would generically settle onto the
   SAME branch, not opposite ones. This is not merely "not addressed by
   this session's search" — it is a real physical tendency working
   AGAINST the mechanism this branch would need, under the most natural
   setup, not a neutral open question.
