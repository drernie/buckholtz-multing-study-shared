## 1. Why "driven non-equilibrium" is a genuinely different question
from what `P162`/`P172`/`P173` already checked

Every prior check in this chain (vector mediator `docs/130`/`131`,
quantum Casimir-Polder `FINDING_P162` §4, scalarization `FINDING_
P172`/`P173`) assumed the interaction is a CONSERVATIVE force derivable
from a static potential `U(s)` — and a general, robust field-theory
fact (informally: stable, ghost-free, positive-energy sources produce
an attractive static potential) blocks all of them for the same root
reason. **Dissipative/non-equilibrium forces are not required to be
conservative** — they need not be expressible as `-∇U(s)` at all, so
this general attractiveness argument does not automatically apply to
them. This is a real, structurally different escape route worth
checking on its own terms, not a re-run of the same static analysis.

## 2. Three real mechanisms checked

**(A) Dissipative self-force / "the inertia of heat" (Herrera &
Pavón)** `[VERIFIED-REAL, direct abstract fetch]`, well-established
relativistic-astrophysics literature (`arXiv:gr-qc/0701073`,
independently re-fetched this session, exact quote below, not a
paraphrase of a search snippet): "the contribution of the inertial term
diminishes the effective inertial mass and the 'gravitational' force
term, **by the same factor**" — "due to the inertia of heat, collapse
may slow down and stop before singularity formation." This is a
genuine, real, dissipation-driven, thermal-energy-tied MODIFICATION of
gravity. **But it SUPPRESSES (weakens) attraction, it does not REVERSE
it to repulsion** — the force stays attractive, just smaller (the
paper's own abstract states the SAME factor reduces both quantities,
not a regime where one could vanish or flip while the other does not —
checked directly against this specific concern, not merely assumed).

**Two related but distinct "does not flip sign" sub-patterns, kept
separate here (corrected — skeptic-caught conflation):** mechanism (A)
here and chameleon/symmetron screening (`FINDING_P171` row 3) both
genuinely WEAKEN an attractive coupling's magnitude without reversing
it — two instances of one sub-pattern. Quantum Casimir-Polder
(`FINDING_P162` §4) and the scalarization/vector-mediator construction
(`docs/130`/`131`, `FINDING_P172`/`P173`) are a DIFFERENT sub-pattern:
full, UNMODIFIED attraction for ordinary positive sources, with no
suggestion the coupling itself is weakened — two instances of that.
These are related (neither sub-pattern reverses sign) but not identical
observations, and the first draft of this file conflated them into one
miscounted "magnitude not sign, N times" tally — corrected throughout.

**(B) Tidal dissipation / secular orbital-separation increase**
`[VERIFIED-REAL]`, textbook astrophysics (the Earth-Moon system is the
standard example, confirmed via WebSearch): tidal dissipation DOES
cause genuine secular INCREASE in orbital separation over time — this
is the one mechanism checked that actually produces the right
*qualitative* outcome (growing separation) via entirely mainstream,
non-exotic physics. **But the physical driver is angular-momentum
transfer via a phase-lagged tidal torque, which REQUIRES an intrinsic
axis** (the spin direction of the tidally-deformed body, offset from
the instantaneous line connecting the pair by the phase lag) — **this
conflicts with v82's own text, quoted precisely (corrected — skeptic-
caught, the first draft dropped v82's own hedge) in `FINDING_P162` §3:
"a node's own thermal energy has no intrinsic axis... one might
consider that the line connecting the two nodes... supplies the
direction, with a node's thermal energy setting only the magnitude."**
The "no intrinsic axis" clause is categorical — v82 flatly denies the
node has its own axis, and that denial alone is what conflicts with
tidal dissipation's own spin-axis requirement. The "line connecting the
nodes supplies the direction" clause is explicitly hedged ("one might
consider") — a suggestion, not an assertion — so the conflict rests on
the categorical denial, not on over-reading the hedged part as a firm
commitment. A structurally different problem from (A): not "right
mechanism, wrong sign," but "right qualitative outcome, wrong required
ingredient (intrinsic axis) v82's own text denies the node has." Also:
in the standard tidal picture, thermal energy is
GENERATED BY the dissipation ("orbital energy is transformed into
thermal energy, which is dissipated") — thermal energy is a
CONSEQUENCE of the process, not its DRIVER, the opposite causal
direction from what MULTING's own `k_A`-sourced dipole needs.

**(C) AGN feedback / thermal-pressure-driven gas ejection**
`[VERIFIED-REAL]`, extensive real literature (multiple sources found):
a genuine, well-studied dissipative, thermal-energy-tied process in
galaxy clusters. **But it operates WITHIN a single cluster's own
potential well** (redistributing/ejecting gas from the cluster center,
"the AGN cannot eject gas from the cluster due to the sheer weight of
material residing in the gravitational potential") — no source found
connecting it to a systematic FORCE ACTING BETWEEN two separate cluster
CENTERS specifically (which is what would be needed to affect the
node-pair separation `s(t)` MULTING's own dipole term acts on).
**Scoped precisely (corrected — skeptic-caught overreach): this is NOT
a claim that AGN feedback has no inter-cluster-scale effects at all** —
baryonic-feedback-on-the-matter-power-spectrum is a real, separate,
well-studied literature (hydrodynamic simulations: EAGLE, IllustrisTNG,
BAHAMAS) showing AGN feedback modifies large-scale clustering on Mpc
scales — but that is a statistical, population-level effect, not a
specific two-body force term between named cluster centers, so it does
not by itself supply what this branch needs either; the "no source
found" claim is narrowed to the specific between-centers-force
question, not AGN feedback's inter-cluster relevance in general.
