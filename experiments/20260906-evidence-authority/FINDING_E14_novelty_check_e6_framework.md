## The candidate, read directly (not from its abstract)

`[VERIFIED-arXiv:2502.10541]` Doboszewski & Elder, *"How Theory-laden
are Observations of Black Holes?"* (2025) — downloaded and read in full
this session. Its Section 6, *"Mitigating the Risks of Theory-laden
Observations,"* identifies **five formal strategies**, quoted directly:

1. **Independent Evidence** — access the quantity through another
   method not sharing the theoretical dependence.
2. **Weakening** — widen the reported uncertainty so the conclusion is
   "compatible with a broader range of theoretical alternatives,"
   citing Ritson & Staley (2021) on ATLAS/HEP uncertainty evaluation
   disarming "the viciousness of inferential circularity."
3. **Explicit** — state the dependence openly rather than hiding it.
4. **Generic Effect** — argue the result would hold under a wide class
   of theories, not just the specific one assumed.
5. **Parameterization** — vary the assumption itself as a free
   parameter and test sensitivity to it.
