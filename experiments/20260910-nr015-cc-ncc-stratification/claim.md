# Claim — NR-015's own named "correct next direction": cool-core /
# non-cool-core stratification to discriminate its two undistinguished
# readings

**L0 (EstimandOps): descriptive.** `NR-015` found `r(delta_M, M_Gas |
M_WL, T_X)` collapses to `-0.08` (CI `[-0.39, 0.27]`) — the raw partial
correlation `r(delta_M, E_ICM | M_WL) ≈ -0.70` is `T_X`-degenerate — but
could not distinguish two readings of WHY:

1. **Definitional-artifact:** `T_X` mechanically enters `M_hydro`'s own
   hydrostatic-equilibrium formula, so controlling for `T_X` removes a
   derivational pathway, not physics — same strength in EVERY cluster,
   relaxed or not.
2. **Common-physical-driver:** cluster dynamical state (merger/relaxation
   stage) drives both elevated `T_X` (shock heating) and larger HSE
   violations (hence `delta_M`) through separate physical channels —
   `T_X` is then a real confound, concentrated in dynamically DISTURBED
   clusters specifically.

**Pre-registered by `NR-015` itself** (Relaxation Map row 2, "cheapest,
correct next direction"): split the same `N=50` CCCP sample at
`K0≈30 keV·cm²` (standard cool-core/non-cool-core cut) and recompute
`r(delta_M, T_X | M_WL)` per subsample.

## Falsifiable predictions

- **Reading (1) predicted pattern:** similar `|r(delta_M, T_X | M_WL)|`
  in both the cool-core (CC) and non-cool-core (NCC) subsamples.
- **Reading (2) predicted pattern:** the correlation concentrated in the
  NCC (dynamically disturbed) subsample, weak or absent in CC (relaxed)
  clusters.

## What this does NOT establish (stated before running)

- Small-`N` subsamples (roughly half of 50 each) will have wide
  confidence intervals — this may be underpowered to give a clean
  verdict, and that itself is a legitimate, informative outcome (not a
  failure to hide).
- Does not by itself quantify `M_hydro`'s actual `T_X` leverage in
  Mahdavi et al. 2013's own pipeline (Relaxation Map row 1, a separate,
  literature-only check) — this test and that one are complementary, not
  substitutes.
