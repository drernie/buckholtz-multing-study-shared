## What this establishes

Across 5 checked niches spanning particle-mass phenomenology (1, 2),
cosmological large-number coincidences (3), classical electrodynamics (4),
and strong-gravity bound states (5), **no existing, already-published
physics result was found that derives or explains Eq.32's specific 4/3
coefficient** in the role Eq.32 uses it (a coefficient in a lepton-mass-
ratio-to-coupling-ratio identity). Niche 4 is the strongest-verified of the
five (two independent, live-rechecked 2024/2025 papers, both directly on
"the 4/3 problem" by name) and is also the most obviously irrelevant on
inspection — both papers' 4/3 factors arise from field-theoretic
